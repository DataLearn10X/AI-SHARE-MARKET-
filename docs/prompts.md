# Phase Prompts – AI Share Market Research Platform

Use these prompts to continue development phase by phase.

## Phase 4 – Complete Fundamental Analysis Engine
Implement comprehensive fundamental analysis: company overview, income/balance/cash flow statements (annual/quarterly/TTM), profitability/valuation/growth/health ratios, financial charts, and transparent interpretation. Use existing data sources, no fabricated data.

## Phase 5 – Stock Comparison, Holdings and Market Events
Implement peer comparison, shareholding pattern, mutual fund holdings, dividends, corporate actions, news/announcements with links, and news sentiment. Use actual reported data; show clear messages when unavailable.

## Phase 6 – AI-Powered Stock Research Engine
Upgrade AI research: business analysis, financial quality, growth, valuation, SWOT, bull/base/bear scenarios, red-flag detection, research summary. Grounded in verified data, cite periods/sources, no invented figures, no personalized advice.

## Phase 7 – Final Quality Assurance and Optimization
Full audit: startup, search, charts, financials, comparisons, shareholding, corporate actions, dividends, news, AI research, missing data, errors, mobile responsiveness, downloads. Verify no hardcoded data, accurate calculations, caching, logs. Provide final report.
